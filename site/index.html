01 · The thesis

Global Weakness, Local Strength

Core claim: unconditional factor screens have a structural blind spot for state-dependent premia. Machines make the supply cheap; the filter still asks the wrong question. This is not a weekend demo — it is a multi-year research program I am on leave to pursue full-time.

One sentence

A factor that pays only inside a rare market state can look globally weak (tg ≈ tstate√π) and be discarded — even when its conditional edge is real.

Long-term agenda
  1. Make the blind spot measurable (Proposition 2 + falsification).
  2. Recover tradeable structure without hard-gating breadth (own-regime tilt).
  3. Industrialize proposal → gate → study-discards for AI-generated factors.
  4. Ship public receipts so outsiders can recompute the load-bearing numbers.
01

Machines flood the zoo

LLMs + search propose predictors faster than humans can vet. Screens become the bottleneck.

02

Unconditional filter

Keep if average premium clears multiple-testing. State-confined premia look like zeros.

03

Structural blind spot

Global t dilutes with √π. Locally strong factors are discarded as globally weak.

04

Own-regime tilt

Per-factor states + hierarchical pooling. Continuous tilt—no hard gate that kills breadth.

05

Falsify, then trade

Block-shuffle nulls, placebos, cross-fit. Then show deployable bps on public panels.

06

Honest tiers

Load-bearing vs identification vs suggestive—said upfront. Science over storytelling.

02 · Mechanism lab

Why the global t goes blind

A premium confined to a paying state of frequency π is diluted in the unconditional mean. Drag the controls — watch the screen discard a factor whose conditional edge never changed.

Unconditional mean

State-frequency-weighted average. Local strength is averaged away by construction.

Blind-spot identity

Proposition 2: global t vanishes as the paying state grows rare—even when conditional t stays large.

Breadth, not leverage

Hard gates raise per-bet skill but destroy effective breadth. Continuous tilt keeps legs and scales weights.
