02 · Mechanism

Why the global t goes blind

A premium confined to a paying state of frequency π is diluted in the unconditional mean. The screen sees weakness; the state sees strength.

Unconditional mean

State-frequency-weighted average. Local strength is averaged away by construction.

Blind-spot identity

Proposition (blind spot): global t vanishes as the paying state grows rare—even when conditional t stays large.

Breadth, not leverage

Hard gates raise per-bet skill but destroy effective breadth. Continuous tilt keeps legs and scales weights.
