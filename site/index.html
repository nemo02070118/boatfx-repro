02b · Resolution limit

The resolution limit

In 1873 Ernst Abbe showed a microscope's sharpest detail is fixed by its aperture, not the grinding of the lens. A universal significance hurdle is the same kind of instrument, and factor discovery has the same kind of limit. How much of a unit's true home-tilt the data recovers obeys one clean attenuation law, q²(τ)=τ/(1+τ) with τ=m/m*, m*≈1.3 factors — negligible for a single factor, nearly complete for a pool. Move the whole discard pool to its home venue once (coarse) and out-of-sample Sharpe goes 0.85 → 1.30, three-quarters of the way to the 1.45 hindsight ceiling. The converse is what makes it a law: no router closes the rest.

Roy (1951) self-selection

Comparative advantage

A global component β_i plus a venue-local component a_iv. Factor-to-venue assignment is a self-selection problem: the screen keeps the global part and discards the local part as noise. On 94 discarded factors the coarse move earns FF6 alpha +3.8%/yr (t=8.3), HXZ q5 +3.4%/yr (t=7.5), increment alpha +2.3%/yr (t=5.1), GRS F(8,594)=5.7 (p<1e-6).

Theorem 1 · resolution law

A diffraction limit, not a wall

The squared overlap between the estimated home-tilt and the truth converges to q²(τ)=τ/(1+τ): negligible as τ→0, exactly one-half at the half-recovery scale m*≈1.3 (τ=1), rising smoothly to one. There is no hard wall — but on the log scale of m the crossover is sharp. The pooled coarse move (m=94, τ≈72) lands at q²≈0.99; per-factor routing (m=1, τ≈0.77) lands at q²≈0.43, below half. Matched to Monte Carlo to three decimals (Appendix IA.2).

The converse · five-router tournament

No router closes the gap

Coarse leaves only 0.14 to the ceiling. Five routers — per-factor argmax, economic-family prior, trailing-performance timing, an ML characteristic map, empirical-Bayes shrinkage — each tuned out of sample and frozen, recover at most 0.05 of it. Routing every factor (fine) loses 0.18. The empirical-Bayes rule, free to shrink, moves zero factors.

Honest ceiling

Coarse is the capacity

The claim is +0.45 Sharpe over current practice (discard = zero), not beating the oracle. Coarse captures 3/4 of the grave→oracle gap, and no method beats it by more than noise. Same wall holds internationally: JKP 47 countries, single-factor venue persistence median 0.23.

Scale law: the discovery hurdle grows like √(2 log N); the allocation threshold like √(2 log log N). The gap diverges — stricter policing of the zoo discards MORE recoverable local strength as N grows.
